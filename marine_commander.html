Phase
Each turn has two phases. In the player phase everyone draws a path for their token, then you press End player turn and all player tokens move together. The enemy phase works the same way for enemy tokens. Shift-drag places any token instantly.

Doors

Click a door on the map to open or close it. A room's lights come on when one of its doors is open.

Lighting

Click a light bulb on the map to cycle it: automatic, forced on, forced off.

Vision & movement

Walls and closed doors stop player tokens and block their sight. Open a door to let them through.
